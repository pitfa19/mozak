# Acknowledgments

MOZAK is an independent implementation. Its source code was written for this
repository and is released under the MIT License.

The project learned from publicly documented ideas, standards, and product
interfaces. In particular:

- [MDKG](https://github.com/nickreames/mdkg) informed Git-authoritative project
  knowledge and read-only cross-repository composition.
- [Tieline](https://github.com/knoxgraeme/tieline) informed the separation of
  contracts, evidence, and derived views.
- [Backstage](https://backstage.io/docs/features/software-catalog/) informed
  stable identities and typed relationships.
- [DVC](https://dvc.org/doc/user-guide/pipelines),
  [MLflow](https://github.com/mlflow/mlflow), and
  [OpenLineage](https://github.com/OpenLineage/OpenLineage) informed research
  run provenance.
- [Graphiti](https://github.com/getzep/graphiti) informed the distinction
  between recorded and valid time.
- [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec) informed explicit resolved
  scope in agent-operated planning.
- [OCI](https://github.com/opencontainers/image-spec),
  [in-toto](https://github.com/in-toto/attestation), and
  [SLSA](https://slsa.dev/) informed digest-addressed package and attestation
  conventions.
- [MOOSEDev](https://github.com/Trivyn/moosedev) informed explicit knowledge
  lifecycle states.
- [i-have-adhd](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd) informed concise,
  action-first human output.

These references acknowledge intellectual influence and interoperability
standards. They do not imply endorsement, compatibility, sponsorship, or that
MOZAK incorporates source code from those projects. Each referenced project
remains governed by its own license and trademarks.

Scientific publications used to justify individual mechanisms are cited at
their points of use and in MOZAK's research evidence. Paper titles, links, and
bibliographic facts are attribution metadata, not incorporated software.
